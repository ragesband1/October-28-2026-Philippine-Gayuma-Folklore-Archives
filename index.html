Philippine Folklore Regional Map & Lore

Documented regional legends, mythical provinces, and traditional esoteric lore across the Philippines.

Region VII (Central Visayas)

Isla de Fuego (Siquijor)

Provinces: Siquijor (San Antonio, Lazi)

Folklore Tradition: Famous worldwide for Holy Week botanical oil brewing rituals. Local Mananambal gather rare herbs, tree barks, roots, and coconut oil under specific moon phases to craft traditional potions.

Application: Herbal infusing & sacred oil aging.
Region VI (Western Visayas)

Antique & Iloilo Hills

Provinces: Antique, Iloilo (Central Panay)

Folklore Tradition: Deep in the mountain ridges, ancient Babaylan lore includes chant-infused botanical concoctions and protective love charms (Habak) combined with specialized aromatic oils.

Application: Charms, aromatic rubbing oil, ritual chants.
Region V (Bicol Region)

Bicol Mystical Belt

Provinces: Camarines Sur, Albay

Folklore Tradition: Local folklore features herbal potions combined with Orasyon (sacred Latin-Tagalog incantations) transcribed onto parchment or photos to invoke lifelong affection and protection.

Application: Photo charms, written Orasyon prayers.

Categories of Traditional Love Potions & Rituals

Exploration of traditional methods documented in Philippine cultural mythology.

1. Pahid (Oil Rub)

Concentrated herbal coconut oil infused with rare vines. Mythically anointed on clothing or skin to induce magnetic attraction.

2. Inumin (Drink)

Boiled herbal tea mixtures brewed under the full moon. Mythically served in small drops in beverages to bind heart felt devotion.

3. Amoy / Spray

Aromatic potion extracts sprayed into the air or worn like perfume to captivate anyone who catches a breath of the mist.

4. Litrato (Photo)

Placing a lover's photograph inside a glass vial wrapped with red thread and special herbs to ensure constant thoughts.

5. Whispered Chant

Sacred Latin-Tagalog whispered prayers spoken over personal belongings to seal eternal affection and companionship.
